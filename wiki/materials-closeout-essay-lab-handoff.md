## Materials service rule

SOURCE ARCHIVE ≠ APP CATALOG. Use LegendStudy's grade-specific **전과목 자료**
source collections to select one representative exam. Exclude obvious language,
국영수, inquiry-only, foreign-language-only and duplicate-format posts from the
normal catalog; preserve sources. No month whitelist. Include a real preliminary
or exceptional sitting when its full-set source exists. Only actual identity,
broken-source or attachment problems are REVIEW_EXCEPTION.

Existing expected-coverage registry/classifier stays available as QA assistance;
it is not an additional approval authority overriding the Owner's source rule.
Do not create another audit phase, classifier, registry expansion, schema or
publication architecture. Historical raw subjects stay raw/unmapped when needed.
Named general files stay `other`; whole-exam answers can remain unscoped resources.
Do not invent a question kind or force old subjects into modern electives.

Pre-launch test references do not block partial cleanup. Prefer simple scoped
inactive state; clear scoped test references only if necessary. No redirect,
reference-migration or merge framework. Do not delete original source/content
provenance. Accepted-state/1710 reconciliation remains separate.

Essay source discovery covers the entire source collection, targeting at least
2020 onward and five or more years of material, preferably 2020–2025. Preserve
source publication dates separately from admission-year labels/ranges. A later
source publication date does not change the admission year of its attached paper.
Keep questions, answers, examples, explanations, rubrics, intentions, combined
PDFs, guides and general university documents. Split question/answer CTA:
**문제 보기 / 답안 보기**; combined/guide/general document: **자료 보기**.
Resource kind alone must not deny a verified PDF. Preserve URL allowlist,
attachment identity and independent PDF evidence; no university-specific hack.

Materials is **not CLOSED until Owner device QA PASS**. After PASS, maintenance
only for actual defects; no autonomous next batch, audit or architecture work.

## Essay LAB Phase0 input and boundaries

Model University → Department/Field → Admission Year/Track → Essay Exam → Resource
→ Question/Passage → Official Criteria/Example Answer. Then connect User Submission
→ AI Feedback → Revision → longitudinal improvement history. Preserve original
LegendStudy PDFs/source identities as corpus inputs, not extracted question claims.

Official university 모집요강 should connect admissions capacity/eligibility,
essay/student-record weights, 수능최저, dates, duration, scope and question format.
Official university results may supply competition and disclosed outcome statistics.
Keep official originals and separately structured/derived data. Do not implement
these schemas, ingestion, AI evaluation, payments or admissions DB during closeout.

University/department/track canonical data must be shared by LAB, onboarding and
MY. Do not add temporary university/department fields to the already accepted
onboarding flow. Preserve the school-search hotfix and Owner device acceptance.

AI feedback is evidence-grounded: actual question/passages, official intentions,
criteria and examples, the student's answer and prior answers/feedback. Identify
what the university actually states separately from LegendStudy/AI interpretation.
Do not present generated analysis as an official assessment criterion.

Target UX: university/field selection → university information → past paper →
write/upload answer → feedback → revise → compare → cumulative growth. Input
formats/OCR implementation are a later scoped decision, not shipped capability.

## Value, privacy and later expansion

Keep **three free feedback experiences** as the product direction: discover a
problem, apply feedback, observe improvement. No payment implementation now;
pricing is a separate decision. Continued use should reflect the value of saved
answers, feedback, targets and preparation history, rather than only quota expiry.

Official documents remain basic access. Separately refined/analysed admissions
data may later be a subscription candidate. Future learning time, grades, target
universities/tracks, submissions, feedback, applications and outcomes can connect
through the [longitudinal strategy](longitudinal-learning-admissions-data-strategy.md).
Collect only currently needed personal data, not speculative future attributes.

B2B may later expose submissions, revisions, progress and university preparation
to appropriately authorized schools/teachers. Do not implement B2B in the B2C MVP;
only avoid model choices that prevent a future consent/authorization design.
User admissions value comes before revenue: usefulness should earn continued use,
payment, support and recommendations.
