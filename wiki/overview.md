# Product Overview

## Purpose

LegendStudy is a native mobile application built around the educational resources published at `legendstudy.com`, especially Korean high-school mock exams and related study materials.

The app should make discovery, viewing, saving, revisiting, and notification of study resources materially easier than using the website alone.

## Core principles

- Native app experience; no simple WebView wrapper
- Fast access to public study resources without mandatory login
- Login reserved mainly for personalization and sync
- Structured search/filtering over normalized content
- Maintain the existing LegendStudy brand identity
- Keep website and app complementary rather than forcing a migration away from `legendstudy.com`

## Project boundary

LegendStudy is independent of Muselry. No code, Supabase project, secrets, OAuth setup, release identifiers, or wiki state should be shared unless a dependency is intentionally introduced and documented as a new decision.

## Intended users

Primary users are Korean high-school students looking for mock-exam, CSAT-related, answer/explanation, listening, and associated study materials.

## Long-term direction

Potential later modules include school/NEIS features, community, achievements, personalized recommendations, and a restored/adapted early-admission prediction service based on the user's prior Selty work. These are not assumed to be v1.0 requirements.
